SHARDFALL - a Belt Patrol adventure
===================================

A light, arcade, cockpit-view space shooter: 8 short missions in a broken
asteroid belt, ending with the Junk Queen. Flat-shaded 3D at 30 FPS on the
Dingoo A320.

Playing
-------
D-pad          steer (Up dives by default: change it in Options)
L / R          roll
A              lasers (hold to keep firing); confirm in menus
B              missile at the locked target; back in menus
X / Y          slower / faster; both together boost
Select         next target
Start          pause (B in the pause menu leaves the mission)

On a PC: arrows, Z = A, X = B, C = X, V = Y, Q / W = L / R, 1 = Start,
2 = Select (the Dingux keys - Ctrl, Alt, Space, Shift, Tab, Backspace,
Enter, Esc - work too).

Keep a target near the crosshair for a second to lock on. Aim at the small
diamond to hit moving ships. Your shield recharges when you stay out of
trouble. Yellow on the radar is where to go next.

Each mission is ranked S, A, B or C on time, accuracy and shield left.
Progress and settings are saved in shardfall.sav next to the game.
Rumour has it that pilots with an A on every mission get to see what's
beyond the belt.

Score Attack
------------
Endless waves, one life. Quick kills build a combo multiplier (up to x8);
clearing a wave earns a bonus and some shield back. Beat the table and enter
your initials. Missiles from the silver ships home in: break hard and they
overshoot.

Options
-------
Pitch control, music and sound volume, detail (LOW draws less and runs
faster), a one-minute PERFORMANCE TEST (it writes shardfall-perf.txt next to
the game), how to play and credits.

Music
-----
Put title.mod, mission.mod and/or boss.mod (4-channel ProTracker modules)
next to the game to replace the built-in music. See CREDITS.txt.
